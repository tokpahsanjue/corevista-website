What file formats can be printed?

Common office documents and PDFs are the starting point. Confirm the file type and layout before work begins.

We can help with typing, formatting, scanning and PDF preparation. Submission requirements should be provided by you.

We can provide practical assistance, but you must review the information and enter your own passwords and verification codes.

Not yet. Prices, hours and payment details will be published only after they are confirmed.
